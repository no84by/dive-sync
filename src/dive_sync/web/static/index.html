Before you start

Experimental. dive-sync is an independent, experimental tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Garmin or SSI (Scuba Schools International). It uses their apps' unofficial interfaces, which they can change or block at any time — when that happens this tool stops working, and it may make mistakes before it notices.

Runs on your computer. Stores nothing. The app runs only on this computer. Your passwords, login sessions and dive data are not saved — they are kept in memory while the app is open and forgotten when you close it. Nothing is sent anywhere except directly to Garmin and SSI. There is no server of ours, no account, no tracking.

No warranty. Provided "as is" under the MIT License, without warranty of any kind. You are responsible for the changes you approve in your logbooks — check the preview before you sync.

A personal one-off, shared in case it helps. No support or follow-up is promised.
